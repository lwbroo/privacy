In Ziwei, export JSON/CSV from inside the app, and delete local profiles and records in the app. In Glyco, your journal stays on your device; delete entries in the app. (3) How do I manage or cancel a Glyco subscription? In iPhone Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions. (4) How do I contact support? Email lwbro@me.com.
