Glyco Navigator Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25. Developer: kurt Chiang — contact: lwbro@me.com. Glyco Navigator is a glucose and meal journal. Your health records stay yours. Glucose readings, meal entries, and any notes you enter are stored only on your device. They are never sent to a server or to any third party. The app does not require an account for its core journal functions. The app does not track you and does not serve third-party advertising. If you contact us at lwbro@me.com, we will use your message only to answer you. We do not sell or share contact messages. Purchases: Glyco Navigator offers a subscription (Pro, unlimited analysis) purchased through the App Store. Payment is processed by Apple; RevenueCat manages your subscription status using an app user identifier. After a purchase or restore, the app syncs your subscription status with our server solely to unlock Pro features. Your glucose and meal records are not included in that sync and remain on your device. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions. Content note: Glyco Navigator is a personal journal, not a medical device and not medical advice. Always consult your clinician for medical decisions. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
