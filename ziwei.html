Ziwei Bazi Trend Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25. Developer: kurt Chiang — contact: lwbro@me.com. Ziwei Bazi Trend is a traditional Chinese fortune-telling culture and entertainment app (Ziwei Doushu and Bazi). It does not require an account, and it does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data. Birth data you enter (year, month, day, time) is used only on your device to generate your chart and related reference content. It is stored only on your device and is never sent to a server or to any third party. The app has no login, no registration, and no cloud sync. The app does not track you, does not serve third-party advertising, and does not use your data for advertising. Your records (profiles, notes, mood/energy logs) stay on your device. You can export them yourself (JSON/CSV) from inside the app; export files go only where you choose to save or send them. If you contact us at lwbro@me.com, we will use your message only to answer you. We do not sell or share contact messages. Content note: chart and annual-flow content is traditional cultural reference for entertainment and self-reflection only. It does not guarantee prediction accuracy and must not be used as the sole basis for medical, financial, legal, or other major life decisions. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
